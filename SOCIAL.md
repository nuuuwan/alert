🚨 Try ALERT: <https://alert.lk>

ALERT is a trilingual, map-based disaster-awareness app for Sri Lanka, built on public data.

Use it to:

- View official alerts and experimental data-driven warnings
- Check current risk information for a selected location
- Explore weather, water levels, air quality, and other useful data
- Find nearby hospitals, police stations, and fire stations
- Access information in English, සිංහල, and தமிழ்

Select your location, explore what is available, and help make it better. Please report bugs and suggest improvements at <https://github.com/nuuuwan/alert/issues>
